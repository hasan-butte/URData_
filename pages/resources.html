A curated list of tools, organizations, legislation, and reporting on data privacy.

Tools & Self-Assessment

  • Cover Your Tracks (EFF)

    Free tool from the EFF that tests how trackable your browser is based on its fingerprint and tracking protections.

  • Have I Been Pwned

    Free service that lets you check if your email address or phone number has appeared in any known data breaches.

  • Privacy Guides

    Community-driven resource recommending privacy-respecting tools and services for browsing, communication, and file storage.

  • Bitwarden

    Free, open-source password manager that generates and stores unique passwords for all your accounts.

  • SimpleLogin

    Email alias service that lets you create unique addresses for each service you sign up for, protecting your real email from being shared or sold.

Organizations & Advocacy Groups

  • Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)

    Nonprofit defending civil liberties in the digital world, including privacy, free expression, and security.

  • Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC)

    Independent research center focused on privacy and civil liberties issues, with a strong record of policy advocacy and government oversight.

  • Access Now

    International organization defending digital rights of users at risk around the world, including data protection and government surveillance.

  • Privacy International

    UK-based charity that investigates and challenges government and corporate surveillance worldwide.

  • Center for Democracy & Technology (CDT)

    Nonpartisan nonprofit shaping technology policy and law, with a focus on privacy, surveillance, and civil rights in the digital age.

Legislation & Policy

  • GDPR Overview (European Commission)

    Overview of the EU's General Data Protection Regulation, the most comprehensive data privacy law in the world.

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)

    California's landmark consumer privacy law giving residents the right to know what data is collected, request deletion, and opt out of data sales.

  • Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA)

    The strongest biometric privacy law in the U.S., requiring consent before collecting fingerprints, faceprints, and other biometric identifiers.

  • FTC Privacy and Data Security Actions

    Searchable database of FTC enforcement actions that include actions related to privacy and data security. Includes cases against notable, large tech companies.

  • California Delete Act (SB 362)

    California law creating a centralized mechanism for consumers to request deletion from multiple data brokers at once.

News & Research

  • The Markup

    Nonprofit newsroom investigating how technology is used to exert power, with a focus on data privacy, algorithms, and surveillance.

  • Wired — Security & Privacy

    Wired's dedicated section covering cybersecurity, data breaches, and digital privacy developments.

  • Ars Technica

    Technology news outlet with in-depth reporting on privacy policy, data collection practices, and surveillance technology.

  • 404 Media

    Independent tech journalism outlet founded by former Vice Motherboard reporters, covering surveillance, hacking, and digital rights.

  • EFF — Cover Your Tracks Research Paper

    Peter Eckersley's foundational research paper on browser fingerprinting, demonstrating that the vast majority of browsers are uniquely identifiable.

  • UCL / UC Davis — Smart TV ACR Tracking Study

    Academic paper auditing Automatic Content Recognition tracking on Samsung and LG smart TVs, revealing the frequency and scope of data collection.

Documentaries & Longform Content

  • The Social Dilemma (2020)

    Netflix documentary featuring former tech executives explaining how social media platforms are designed to exploit user behavior for profit.

  • Citizenfour (2014)

    Academy Award-winning documentary following Edward Snowden's disclosure of NSA mass surveillance programs.

  • The Great Hack (2019)

    Documentary exploring the Cambridge Analytica scandal and how personal data was weaponized for political manipulation.

  • Terms and Conditions May Apply (2013)

    Documentary examining how corporations and governments exploit the terms of service that nobody reads.

  • Nothing to Hide (2017)

    Documentary challenging the common argument that "if you have nothing to hide, you have nothing to fear."
